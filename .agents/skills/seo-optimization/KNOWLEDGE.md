# Review optimization evidence

Use `SKILL.md` for the workflow. Recheck time-sensitive eligibility, feature availability and rollout status before recommending a change. A previous review date is not evidence that a rule remains current.

## Current behavior and project evidence

Keep documented behavior, project observations and proposed experiments distinguishable. For each consequential recommendation, identify the affected pages, evidence date, scope, uncertainty and a measurable acceptance condition. A tactic reported to work elsewhere remains a hypothesis for this website. Do not convert repetition or an apparent correlation into a ranking requirement.

Use current Google documentation for Google behavior and policy. Resolve conflicting versions using their dates, affected feature and newer explicit guidance. A documentation change does not itself announce an algorithm rollout. Keep missing access explicit and do not substitute a search snippet or a video title for inspected evidence.

## Added value before scale

Before expanding content, identify the reader's task and the original contribution each page provides. Reuse an existing page when it already serves that task. Test a representative sample and review facts, business details and navigation before broader publication. Approval to generate drafts does not authorize publishing them. A page for every query variation or location is not automatically a useful strategy.

## Separate AI visibility measures

Check effective Search generative AI inclusion in Search Console, including inherited choices. Keep this account observation separate from crawlability and indexed/snippet eligibility. The generative performance report documents impressions. Verify the available export and binding before requesting metrics. Sampled answers, impressions, visits and business actions answer different questions. Do not derive one from another or assume access through the existing Search Analytics adapter.

## Updates and follow-up

Verify rollout start, completion, timezone and affected product in the official status record. Correlate dates with the project's actual changes, queries, pages, devices and markets before proposing a cause. For core-update analysis, Google recommends waiting at least a full week after completion before comparing performance. This is an analysis interval, not a recovery promise or a reason to delay fixing an independently confirmed defect.

At this review, the ranking history lists the May 2026 core update as completed on June 2, 2026. The September 2026 spam update ran from September 24 through October 8, 2026, globally across languages. Status timestamps use US/Pacific. These are dated observations, not a permanent list of the latest updates. Reopen the status records for later work. Do not predict an update from the time elapsed since the previous one.

Google's May 15, 2026 documentation clarification says spam policies also cover manipulation of generative responses. Its FAQ rich-result retirement took effect May 7, 2026. Reassess affected recommendations without assuming that every traffic change resulted from either event.

## Checklist assessment

Use [the scoring guide](../../../docs/SCORING.md) and [content review](../seo-onpage-checklist/CONTENT_REVIEW.md) to assess a page or site. The rubric is a kit decision, not a Google metric. Itemized review preserves missing evidence and explains applicability. Keep the page's actual purpose and conditional tasks visible when comparing proposed changes. A higher count of passing easy checks does not establish a better search outcome.
